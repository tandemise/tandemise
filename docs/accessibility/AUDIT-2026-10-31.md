# Desktop accessibility audit, 2026-10-31 (WCAG 2.1 AA)

The owner's bar for the desktop app is WCAG 2.1 AA. This audit was taken in the
real window, not from the source: a fresh `TANDEMISE_HOME`, the daemon from
this checkout, `electron-vite dev` over CDP, and the neutral demo data of
`scratch/docs-screenshots/seed.mjs` (project "Taskly", owner "Sam", four agents
on a scripted runtime), so every screen had content.

## Method

Every screen and state, in the light and the dark theme:

Home · Board (board, intake) · Missions (in progress, backlog, routines, the
routine dialog) · New mission · a mission's
Feed, Plan, Timeline, Artifacts, Checks and Metrics tabs · the task drawer ·
the cancel confirmation · a mission stopped at its limit (and its metrics) ·
a mission being made ready · a stalled mission · Inbox (and stalled) ·
Artifacts and the reader · Repositories · Team (people, staffing, roles) ·
Skills · Evals (suites, runs, yours) · Runtimes · Integrations · Brain (sync,
lessons, proposals, factory) · Settings (appearance, notifications, team
relay, intake, daemon) · the command palette · first run (no project) · the
daemon-down screen · the held step's terminal panel (hold suite, H12).

On each, `scratch/acceptance/a11y/run-all.mjs` ran:

- **axe-core 4.14** with the `wcag2a`, `wcag2aa`, `wcag21a` and `wcag21aa` tags;
- **structure**: exactly one `main`, exactly one `h1`, no skipped heading level,
  a navigation landmark, a document title;
- **names**: every button, link, tab and field has an accessible name;
- **keyboard**: Tab from the top of the document until focus comes back to the
  start, asserting the skip link is first, a focus indicator at every stop, the
  stop on screen, and no cycle short of the whole page (a trap); in a dialog,
  focus stays inside and Escape closes it;
- **reflow** at 320 CSS px wide (1280 at 400%, 1.4.10) and at 640 (200%,
  1.4.4): no horizontal page scroll, no content area scrolling in two
  dimensions, no text past the right edge;
- **text spacing** (1.4.12): the WCAG overrides applied, no text clipped;
- **status messages** (4.1.3): live-region updates counted for 20 s on a
  mission with an agent working.

Per-screen counts before and after are in
`docs/superpowers/evidence/2026-10-31-wcag/` (`BEFORE.md`, `before.json`,
`REPORT.md`, `results.json`, and the side by side `COMPARISON.md`), with
focus and 320 px screenshots in `shots/`. Before: 255 axe nodes and 202 of 448
checks failing. After: 0 axe nodes and 0 of 470 checks failing (the after run
also opens the first-run and daemon-down windows).

## Gates

- `node scratch/acceptance/a11y/run-all.mjs --keep-painting`: every check
  above on every screen and state, light and dark; any problem fails it. CI
  runs it as **Accessibility (desktop)** under xvfb.
- Hold suite **H12**: the terminal panel.
- `npm run check:design` rule 11: an icon-only control without
  `aria-label`/`aria-labelledby` fails the build.
- `npm run -w @tandemise/design storybook:a11y`: every story, axe, both themes,
  with play tests for the skip link and the input focus ring.

## Notes

- The plan diagram (#5) is the one place that scrolls in two dimensions at
  320 px; WCAG 1.4.10 exempts content that needs a two-dimensional layout, and
  the same plan reads linearly, in order, by keyboard and screen reader.
- The window's own minimum size (960 x 600) is main-process policy and was
  not changed; the reflow above covers it zoomed in (⌘+) to 400%.
